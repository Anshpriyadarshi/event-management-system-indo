# Logistics Manager

## Overview

The Logistics Manager is responsible for managing the physical arrangements, resources, transportation, and supplies required for the successful execution of an event.

## Responsibilities

- Arrange and prepare the event venue.
- Manage seating arrangements and venue setup.
- Coordinate transportation and parking facilities.
- Arrange required equipment and event materials.
- Coordinate with suppliers and vendors.
- Manage food, refreshments, and other supplies.
- Ensure all required materials are delivered on time.
- Handle setup and cleanup activities.
- Support the Event Manager during the event.

## Logistics Requirements

| Category | Requirements |
|----------|--------------|
| Venue | Seating, stage, tables, chairs |
| Transportation | Vehicles, parking, guest transport |
| Equipment | Projectors, speakers, cables |
| Food | Catering, snacks, refreshments |
| Supplies | Stationery, badges, banners |
| Decoration | Lights, decorations, furniture |

## Event-Day Responsibilities

- Ensure the venue is ready before the event.
- Check that all equipment and materials are available.
- Coordinate with vendors and delivery teams.
- Manage transportation and parking.
- Handle unexpected logistical problems.
- Ensure smooth movement of people and materials.

## Workflow

1. Identify logistics requirements.
2. Prepare the required resources list.
3. Coordinate with vendors and suppliers.
4. Arrange transportation and venue setup.
5. Check all materials before the event.
6. Manage logistics during the event.
7. Handle post-event cleanup and return of equipment.
