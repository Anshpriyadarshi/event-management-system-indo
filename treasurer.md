The Treasurer is responsible for managing the financial activities of the event. This includes preparing the budget, tracking expenses, managing income, and maintaining accurate financial records.

Responsibilities

- Prepare and manage the event budget.
- Track all event-related expenses.
- Record income from registrations, sponsorships, and other sources.
- Approve or review event expenses.
- Maintain financial records and receipts.
- Coordinate with the event manager and sponsorship team.
- Monitor the remaining budget.
- Ensure expenses stay within the approved budget.
- Prepare financial reports after the event.

Sources of Income

- Event registration fees
- Sponsorships
- Donations
- Ticket sales
- Merchandise sales
- Other event income

## Expense Tracking

Every expense should be recorded with the following details:

- Expense ID
- Date
- Description
- Category
- Amount
- Payment method
- Person/vendor
- Receipt or proof of payment

 Financial Workflow

1. Prepare the initial event budget.
2. Estimate expected income and expenses.
3. Allocate the budget to different departments.
4. Record all incoming funds.
5. Track every event expense.
6. Review and approve expenses.
7. Compare actual expenses with the planned budget.
8. Prepare the final financial report.
9. Record the remaining balance or deficit.

Financial Report

At the end of the event, the Treasurer should prepare a report containing:

- Total income
- Total expenses
- Planned budget
- Actual spending
- Remaining balance
- Outstanding payments
- Sponsorship income
- Final financial summary
